1. Introduction

Welcome to Chesstickle. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

When you use Chesstickle, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Authentication Data: If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture provided by Google.
  • Gameplay Data: We store your game states, moves, power-up usage, and matchmaking data in our Firebase Realtime Database to facilitate online multiplayer functionality.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • Match you with other players for online games.
  • Synchronize game state (moves, power-ups) between devices in real-time.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services (for authentication)
  • Google Firebase (for real-time database and analytics)

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated gameplay data, please contact us at the email provided below.
